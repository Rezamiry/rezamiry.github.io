**Problem.** Policy and claims knowledge was spread across many documents, and Customer Service staff needed fast, trustworthy answers.

**Approach.** I built a RAG system using Azure AI Search for retrieval, GPT-4 for generation and Document Intelligence for parsing complex documents, with grounding and evaluation built in.

**Result.** It was the enterprise's first production RAG system and became the primary reference tool for the Customer Service team.
